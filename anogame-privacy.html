「あのゲーム」（以下「本アプリ」）は、Yuki株式会社（以下「当方」）が提供します。本アプリは、遊ぶために氏名やメールアドレスの登録を求めません。このポリシーは、本アプリが実際に扱う情報だけを、扱う順に説明するものです。

01アカウントについて

本アプリにはサインアップ画面がありません。初回起動時に匿名アカウントが自動的に作られ、ランダムなID（UUID）が1つ割り当てられます。このIDにメールアドレス、パスワード、電話番号は一切結び付いていません。

このIDは、対戦相手とのマッチングと、あなたの対戦を他人の対戦と区別するためだけに使われます。

ご注意：ログイン手段がないため、アプリのデータを消去した場合・アプリを削除した場合・ブラウザの保存データを消した場合、そのアカウントは復元できません。名前、戦績、所持している技はそこで失われます。

02当方のサーバーに保存される情報

プロフィール

  • あなたが入力した表示名（12文字以内）
  • 選んだカラーと構え（アバター）
  • 作成日時・更新日時

表示名・カラー・構えは、対戦画面で相手プレイヤーに表示されます。本名や、他人に知られたくない情報を表示名に入力しないでください。

対戦記録

  • 対戦した2人のアカウントID、ライフとエネルギーの残り、ターン数、勝敗
  • 各ターンで選ばれた手と、その解決結果
  • 対戦の開始・終了時刻、マッチング待機の記録

対戦中に選んだ手は、両者が選び終わるまで相手からは読み取れない領域に保存されます。これはゲームの公平性のための仕組みであり、外部に公開されることはありません。

03端末の中だけに保存される情報

次の情報は、あなたの端末の中に保存されるだけで、当方のサーバーには送信されません。

  • 音量などの各種設定、表示言語
  • 戦績（対戦数・勝敗数）、経験値やレベルなどの進行状況
  • スタミナの残量と回復時刻
  • デッキ構成と保存したデッキ
  • 広告の表示間隔を調整するためのカウンター（何試合終えたか、直近の表示時刻）

表示言語の初期値を決めるために、端末の言語設定を読み取ります。読み取るのは言語の種類だけで、記録も送信もしません。

04広告について

本アプリは、Google の AdMob を通じて広告を配信しています。表示されるのは、画面下部のバナー広告、対戦終了後のインタースティシャル広告、そしてスタミナと引き換えにあなたが自分で選んで再生するリワード広告です。

AdMob は広告の配信・表示回数の計測・不正クリックの防止のため、当方を経由せずに次のような情報を取得します。

  • 広告識別子（Android の広告ID、iOS の IDFA）
  • IPアドレス、おおよその地域
  • 端末の機種、OSのバージョン、言語設定
  • 広告の表示・クリックの記録

これらの情報の取り扱いは Google のプライバシーポリシーに従います：policies.google.com/privacy（AdMob におけるデータ利用についてはこちら）。

同意と、広告を減らす方法

  • EU・英国・スイスの方：初回起動時に Google のユーザーメッセージングプラットフォーム（UMP）による同意画面を表示します。パーソナライズ広告に同意しない選択ができ、その場合はパーソナライズされていない広告が表示されます。
  • iOS の方：同意画面のあとに、Apple のトラッキング許可（ATT）を求めます。「許可しない」を選んでも本アプリは通常どおり遊べます。
  • 広告IDのリセット・無効化：Android は「設定 → プライバシー → 広告」、iOS は「設定 → プライバシーとセキュリティ → トラッキング」からいつでも変更できます。
  • 広告そのものを消す：ショップの「広告オフ」をご購入いただくと、バナー広告とインタースティシャル広告が表示されなくなります。

05購入について

AnoGame (“the app”) is made by Yuki K.K. (“we”). The app never asks for your name or your email address in order to play. This policy describes only what the app actually handles, in the order it handles it.

01Your account

There is no sign-up screen. The first time you open the app it creates an anonymous account and assigns it one random ID (a UUID). No email address, password, or phone number is attached to that ID.

The ID is used only to pair you with an opponent and to tell your matches apart from everyone else's.

Worth knowing: because there is nothing to sign back in with, clearing the app's data, deleting the app, or clearing your browser storage loses the account for good. Your name, your record, and the techniques you have unlocked go with it.

02What we store on our servers

Your profile

  • The display name you typed (up to 12 characters)
  • The colour and stance you picked
  • When the profile was created and last changed

Your name, colour, and stance are shown to your opponent during a match. Please don't use your real name, or anything you would not want another player to read.

Match records

  • The two account IDs in the match, lives and energy remaining, turn number, winner
  • The move chosen each turn and how it resolved
  • When the match started and ended, and matchmaking queue entries

The move you pick during a turn is held where your opponent cannot read it until you have both committed. That is a fairness mechanism in the game; it is never published.

03What stays on your device

The following is kept on your device only and is never sent to our servers:

  • Your settings, including volumes and language
  • Your record (matches, wins, losses) and your level and experience
  • Your stamina and when it next refills
  • Your deck and any decks you have saved
  • Counters that pace the ads — how many matches you have finished, when one last showed

The app reads your device's language setting once, to decide which language to start in. It reads the language and nothing else, and does not record or transmit it.

04Advertising

The app shows ads through Google AdMob: a banner at the bottom of the screen, an interstitial after a match ends, and rewarded videos that you choose to watch in exchange for stamina.

To serve those ads, measure them, and detect click fraud, AdMob collects the following directly — it does not pass through us:

  • Your advertising identifier (Advertising ID on Android, IDFA on iOS)
  • Your IP address and approximate location
  • Device model, OS version, and language
  • Records of which ads were shown and clicked

Google handles this under its own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy (and specifically for how AdMob uses data).

Consent, and how to see fewer ads

  • In the EEA, UK, and Switzerland: on first launch the app shows Google's User Messaging Platform consent form. You can refuse personalised ads, and you will be shown non-personalised ones instead.
  • On iOS: after that form, the app asks for Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission. Choosing “Ask App Not to Track” does not affect the game.
  • Reset or disable your advertising ID: Android — Settings → Privacy → Ads. iOS — Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Remove the ads entirely: the “ads off” purchase in the shop stops the banner and the interstitial.

05Purchases

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple's App Store or Google Play. We never see or receive your card number or any other payment details. All we learn is whether a purchase went through.

06Who else receives data

The app runs on these services:

Google AdMob Serves the ads. Privacy policy
Supabase Stores accounts, profiles, and match records. Privacy policy
Vercel Hosts the web version. Privacy policy
Apple / Google Distribute the app and process payments, under their own policies.

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with anyone else for any purpose other than the advertising described above, except where the law requires us to disclose it.

These providers' servers may be located outside your country. By using the app you agree to your information being transferred and stored there.

07How long we keep it

  • Accounts and profiles are kept until you ask us to delete them.
  • Match records are kept so matches can be resumed and cheating can be checked, and are deleted along with the account they belong to.
  • Anything on your device is gone the moment you delete the app.

08Your rights

You can ask us to do any of the following, at the address at the bottom of this page:

  • Delete your account, your profile, and your match records.
  • Send you a copy of what we hold about you.
  • Correct it — your display name can be changed in the app at any time.

Because there is no login, a deletion request must include the account ID shown in the app. Without an ID we can match, we cannot identify the account and cannot act on the request.

Clearing the app's data on your device removes everything held on the device immediately, without asking us.

09Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If we learn that we hold information from a child under 13, we will verify and delete it. Parents and guardians who believe this has happened should contact us at the address below.

10Security

All traffic to our servers is encrypted. Match records are protected by row-level security, so a player can only read matches they took part in. No transmission or storage over the internet is ever completely secure.
